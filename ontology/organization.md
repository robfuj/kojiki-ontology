# Ontology

ORGANIZATION → ORGANIZATIONAL LINE → FUNCTION → ROLE → DECISION → ACTION → EVIDENCE → OUTCOME → LEARNING → RULE / DECISION STRUCTURE

## Organization
The complete economic and operating system.
- Orientation question: *What system are we modeling?*
## Organizational Line
A major business capability such as Sales, Finance, Product, or Legal.
- Orientation question: *Which domain owns this problem?*
## Function
A specific responsibility inside a line.
- Orientation question: *What work must be performed?*
## Role
A person or agent with authority/capability.
- Orientation question: *Who can act or decide?*
## Decision
A choice among alternatives under stated conditions.
- Orientation question: *What choice must be made?*
## Action
The execution resulting from a decision.
- Orientation question: *What happens next?*
## Evidence
Data, observations, facts, tests, or signals used to support/refute a decision.
- Orientation question: *What do we actually know?*
## Outcome
What happened after action.
- Orientation question: *Did reality match expectation?*
## Learning
An extracted pattern, correction, or insight from an outcome.
- Orientation question: *What changed our understanding?*
## Rule / Decision Structure
Reusable logic derived from evidence and learning.
- Orientation question: *How should future decisions change?*
