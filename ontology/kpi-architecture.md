# KPI Architecture

## Activity
- Purpose: How much work occurred?
- Example: Calls, tickets, analyses, releases
## Quality
- Purpose: Was the work done well?
- Example: Accuracy, defect rate, response quality
## Outcome
- Purpose: Did it create the intended result?
- Example: Revenue, retention, margin, reliability
## Leading indicator
- Purpose: What may predict the future?
- Example: Adoption, pipeline quality, risk signals
## Lagging indicator
- Purpose: What already happened?
- Example: Churn, realized margin, incidents
## Decision quality
- Purpose: Was the decision itself good given available evidence?
- Example: Forecast calibration, win/loss, investment outcome
