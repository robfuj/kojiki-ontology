# Learning Taxonomy (docx S8)

## 01 Signal
An early observable indicator that may predict an outcome.
## 02 Pattern
A repeated relationship across cases.
## 03 Cause
A demonstrated or strongly supported explanation for an outcome.
## 04 Assumption
A belief that was necessary for a decision.
## 05 Decision
Whether a decision was correct, incorrect, or unresolved.
## 06 Action
Whether an action produced the intended response.
## 07 Exception
A case that does not fit the current rule or model.
## 08 Failure
A material breakdown and its failure mode.
## 09 Success
A repeatable explanation for an unusually good outcome.
## 10 Rule
A reusable decision principle supported by evidence.
## 11 Threshold
A decision boundary that should change based on evidence.
## 12 Automation
A decision or workflow that can be safely automated.
## 13 Escalation
A condition requiring higher authority or cross-functional review.
## 14 Memory
Knowledge the organization should retain permanently.
